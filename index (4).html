Distance table and how it works
  1. Start. The gate gets distance 0. Every other node gets infinity.
  2. Settle. Take the unsettled node with the smallest distance from the priority queue. That distance is final.
  3. Relax. For each open aisle or free slot next to it, keep the shorter of the old distance and the new one through this node.
  4. Stop. The first free slot to be settled is the closest one. Parked cars are skipped, closed aisles are ignored, and traffic multiplies an aisle's length.
